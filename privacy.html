Privacy Policy — Whisker Window Uploader

Last updated: 2026-10-17

Who uses it

Whisker Window Uploader is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Whisker Window Cat TV, on the owner's own computer, for the owner's own channel.

What it accesses

  • YouTube upload and manage (youtube.upload, youtube): to upload the channel's videos and set their metadata, thumbnails and playlists.
  • YouTube Analytics, read-only (yt-analytics.readonly): to read the channel's own performance reports.

What it stores and shares

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. Analytics numbers are saved as private notes for the owner. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from other people is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at Google Account → Third-party connections.

This tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Via the channel's YouTube page: youtube.com/@whiskerwindowcat.
